# Sound credits

Every clip in this folder is cut from public-domain (CC0 1.0) recordings.
`tools/build-audio.mjs` shows exactly which file and segment each clip comes from.

| Clips | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pistol_*`, `shotgun_*`, `rifle_*` (Bersa .380, Benelli Nova, AK-47) | The Free Firearm Sound Library, mirror: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary | Still North Media | CC0 1.0 |
| `zombie_*` | Zombies Sound Pack, https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack | artisticdude | CC0 1.0 |
| `step_grass_*`, `step_hard_*`, `step_wood_*`, `hit_flesh_*`, `hit_wall_*`, `hit_metal_*`, `hit_wood_*` | Impact Sounds, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `step_gravel_*`, `step_leaves_*`, `step_mud_*` | Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud, https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud | opengameart.org | CC0 1.0 |
| `knife_hit_*`, `player_hurt_*` | 37 Hits/Punches, https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches | qubodup | CC0 1.0 |

Kenney and OpenGameArt files were taken from the CC0 index
https://github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index.
